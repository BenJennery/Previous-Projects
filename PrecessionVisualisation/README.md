Project was to investigate how a distributed mass behaves differently to a point mass. As shown, elliptically orbiting satelites precess around the the central mass. The curves shown at the lower half of the diagram show the differnce between the acceleration of point assumtion mass and the distributed mass (mass masses, one central and 6 on the edge). The differnce graph shows how measuring the acceleration of satalites can allow us to calculate the earths density distribution.
